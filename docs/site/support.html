Learn-Quize Support

Questions, bug reports, or a wrong answer in a quiz? Email samir.tak@dynatechconsultancy.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

  • A question's answer looks wrong. Tap the flag icon on that question during a quiz — it goes straight to our review queue.
  • I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password? Reset it" and follow the email link.
  • My streak reset unexpectedly. Streaks roll over at midnight in your device's timezone; finishing at least one quiz a day keeps them alive.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account, or see the account deletion page.
