Learn-Quize Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

Learn-Quize is a quiz practice app made by Dynatech. This page describes every piece of data the app handles. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name as you enter it, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Practice activity: the quizzes you take — answers, scores, streaks, and the review schedule the app builds from your mistakes.
  • Device timezone: so your daily streak rolls over at your midnight, not UTC's.

That is the whole list. No contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no usage analytics.

How it is used

To run the app: signing you in, tracking your scores and streak, scheduling reviews of the questions you missed, and ranking the weekly leaderboard.

What other users can see

Only the leaderboard is shared: your display name and your average quiz score are visible to other signed-in users once you have finished three or more quizzes. Your answers, history and email are never visible to anyone but you.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and is protected by row-level security — each account can read only its own records.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything attached to it at any time, in the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. See the account deletion page for details, or email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Learn-Quize is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
