Delete your Learn-Quize account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Learn-Quize and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It erases your account, profile, quiz history, scores, streaks, review schedule and leaderboard entry — everything associated with your email. Nothing is retained.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email samir.tak@dynatechconsultancy.com from the address you registered with and we will delete the account for you within 30 days.
